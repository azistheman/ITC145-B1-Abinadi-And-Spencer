What is a TV?

A Television is a machine that transmits visual content, no sound or color, that is the the bare minimum of a television. But of course you won't really find a television that doesn't have color or sound nowadays, but just with those chanegs alone it's showing you how interesting the changes of televisons can be

terms to know:

  • TV screen definitions: Cathode-Ray Tube (CRT)
  • Digital Light Processing (DLP)
  • Plasma Display Panel (PDP)
  • Liquid-Crystal-Display (LCD)
  • Organic Light-Emitting Diode (OLED)
  • Display Resolutions: Low-Definition (LD)
  • Standard-Definition (SD)
  • Enhanced-Definition (ED)
  • High-Definition (HD)
  • Ultra-High-Definition (UHD)
  • Some Aspect Ratios: 4:3 and 16:9 ratios, 480p/1080p and 4K, 8K, 16K, Portrait, Widescreen.
